Tower of Mana

A procedurally-driven roguelike action dungeon crawler.

About the Game

Tower of Mana is a roguelike action dungeon crawler where players ascend a mysterious tower in search of answers surrounding their father's disappearance.

Each run through the tower is procedurally driven, ensuring that no two playthroughs are the same. Combat emphasizes timing, positioning, and decision-making as enemies grow stronger while players climb higher.

Success depends on mastering movement, dodging attacks, and adapting to the challenges presented on each floor.

My Role

Tower of Mana was developed by a six-person team over six months using Unity Engine during the COVID-19 lockdown period. I served as the team lead while also contributing directly to the game's programming.

Team Lead

I led the six-person development team throughout the six-month project and coordinated the development of the game during the COVID-19 lockdown period.

Room Generation

I programmed the procedural room spawning system used to construct the dungeon layouts encountered during each run.

Gameplay

Players ascend the Tower of Mana while fighting increasingly challenging enemies and navigating procedurally generated dungeon layouts.

The procedural structure creates variation between runs, requiring players to adapt their movement and combat approach as they progress through the tower.

Gameplay Showcase

Room Spawning System

One of my primary programming contributions to Tower of Mana was the room spawning system used to generate the game's dungeon layout.

The system selects room positions within a defined grid, chooses directions for expansion, validates that generated rooms remain within the allowed boundaries, and then constructs the final room layout using predefined room components.
