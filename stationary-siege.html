Stationary Siege

A 2D tower defense game set on a student's desk.

About the Game

Stationary Siege is a 2D tower defense game where the player takes the role of Javen, a student dreaming of defending his pencil case from an army of sentient stationery on his desk.

In this miniature tabletop world, players build and upgrade turrets to stop incoming enemies while managing the amount of noise they create. Make too much noise and the teacher may notice and confiscate your defenses.

My Role

Stationary Siege was developed by an eight-person team over six months using a custom 2D game engine. I served as the team lead while contributing to both the engine and the game's systems and level design.

Team Lead

I led the eight-person development team throughout the six-month project and coordinated the development of both the custom engine and the game.

Engine Serialization

I worked on the custom engine's serialization system, supporting the storage and reconstruction of game data used throughout the project.

Upgrade System

I developed the game's turret upgrade system, allowing players to select and improve their placed defenses during gameplay.

Level Design

I contributed to the level design of Stationary Siege, designing playable spaces around the game's tower defense mechanics.

Gameplay

Players defend their pencil case by placing turrets around the level to stop waves of invading stationery. Turrets can be selected during gameplay and upgraded or destroyed depending on the player's strategy.

Alongside the traditional tower defense mechanics, players must also manage noise. Creating too much noise risks attracting the teacher's attention and having their defenses confiscated.

Controls

Left Mouse Button Interact with buttons, place turrets and select placed turrets.
Right Mouse Button Deselect the currently selected turret.
ESC Pause the game.
F1 Toggle the FPS display.
F11 Toggle between fullscreen and windowed mode.
