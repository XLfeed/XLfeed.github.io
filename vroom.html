A VIRTUAL INTRODUCTION

My VRoom

An interactive virtual tour designed to introduce visitors to who I am.

Solo Academic Project • 2 Weeks • Android

Application Demo

Explore My VRoom through this demonstration of its virtual environment and interactive features.

About My VRoom

My VRoom is a virtual reality application created as a personal introduction to its developer.

Developed independently over two weeks during the COVID-19 period, the project explores an interactive approach to presenting information about myself.

Rather than navigating a traditional profile page, users can look around a virtual environment and discover additional information through interactive icons.

The application was designed as part of an academic project and is distributed as an Android APK.

My Role

My VRoom was developed as a solo academic project, giving me responsibility for creating the virtual introduction experience.

Experience Design

Created the concept of introducing myself through an explorable virtual environment rather than a conventional presentation.

Camera Navigation

Developed an experience that allows users to explore their surroundings through device orientation or touchscreen input.

Interactive Information

Incorporated information icons that users can tap to expand additional content within the application.

Independent Development

Completed the academic project independently within a two-week development period.

How to Explore

My VRoom offers two ways to look around the virtual environment. Users can also interact with information icons to discover more content.

01

Gyroscope

Move or rotate your device to change the camera's view.

02

Touchscreen

Drag your finger across the screen to pan the camera manually.

03

Information Icons

Tap the "i" icons to expand information about the corresponding items.
