About the Game

Majestic Mess is a stealth game where the player takes the role of a thief secretly hired by the court wizard to steal the king's most valuable treasures.

The king has developed a strange obsession with collecting eccentric junk from across the world. While the king considers these objects treasures, the frustrated court wizard sees the castle slowly turning into a junkyard.

Armed with the wizard's magical abilities, the player must infiltrate the castle, avoid its guards and make their way towards the treasures hidden within.

My Role

Majestic Mess was developed by an eleven-person team using a custom C++ game engine. I served as the team lead and lead game designer while also contributing directly to the technical development of the engine.

Team Lead

I led the eleven-person development team, coordinating the project across both the design and technical sides of development.

Lead Game Designer

I led the game's overall design direction, including the stealth mechanics, enemy detection behaviour, alertness system and disguise mechanic.

C# / C++ Scripting Bridge

I developed the bridge between the custom C++ engine and C# scripting layer, allowing gameplay behaviour written in C# to interact with systems provided by the engine.

Engine Systems

I contributed to the custom engine by implementing its collision system and shadow rendering functionality.

Gameplay

Players explore the castle while avoiding enemy guards and searching for the king's treasures. Staying undetected is important, as enemies become increasingly alert when they spot the player.

The player can sneak through the environment, hide and use magical abilities provided by the wizard to approach encounters in different ways.

The player's objective is to navigate the castle, steal its major treasures and escape while managing the threat posed by the castle's guards.

Gameplay Showcase

Design & Development

Stealth & Enemy Detection

I designed Majestic Mess's stealth mechanics around avoiding enemy detection while navigating the castle. Enemies can spot the player and progressively become more alert as they maintain detection.

Once an enemy fully detects the player, nearby enemies are alerted and begin pursuing the player as well. This turns detection by a single enemy into a larger threat and encourages the player to remain aware of enemy positioning while navigating the level.

Disguise Mechanic

I also designed the disguise ability associated with one of the game's major treasures. The treasure is a mask that allows the player to transform into an enemy knight, providing another way for the player to approach stealth encounters.

Technical Development

C# / C++ Scripting Bridge

Majestic Mess was developed using a custom C++ game engine with gameplay behaviour written through C# scripts. I worked on the bridge connecting the C# scripting layer to the C++ engine, allowing gameplay scripts to access and interact with engine functionality.

Collision System

I worked on the engine's physics functionality by implementing its collision system, providing the collision behaviour required by the game and its environment.

Shadow Rendering

I also implemented shadow functionality within the custom engine, contributing to the rendering systems used to produce the game's final visual presentation.
