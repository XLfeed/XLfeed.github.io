The Dungeon

An endless horde survival game built around combat and upgrades.

About the Game

The Dungeon is an endless horde survival game where the player must survive against waves of enemies for as long as possible.

As the game progresses, the player can upgrade themselves to improve their combat capabilities and increase their chances of surviving the increasingly dangerous encounter.

My Role

The Dungeon was developed by a five-person team over one month. I served as the team lead while also contributing directly to the game's combat and upgrade systems.

Team Lead

I led the five-person development team throughout the one-month project and helped coordinate the development of the game.

Player Attacks

I worked on the player's attack systems, contributing to the combat mechanics used to fight enemies during each run.

Upgrade System

I developed the game's upgrade system, allowing the player to improve their capabilities as they progress through a run.

Gameplay

Players move through the dungeon while surviving against continuously attacking enemies. The objective is to remain alive for as long as possible.

Progression is driven by upgrades that improve the player's capabilities throughout the run, helping them survive against the increasingly dangerous horde.
