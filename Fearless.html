Fearless

A 2D puzzle platformer about finding hope and safety in a world consumed by darkness.

Singapore Polytechnic Client Project • AFA Singapore 2019

A Journey Through Darkness

About the Game

Fearless is a 2D puzzle platformer developed for Android, following the journey of Seika, a young girl determined to save her world from a spreading darkness.

As the darkness consumes the land, Seika must journey towards a prophesied altar and restore the light needed to dispel the blight.

The game challenges players to navigate through dangerous environments by using light sources to create safe pathways and solve environmental puzzles.

Players must find a key and bring it to the exit of each level, all while avoiding prolonged exposure to the surrounding darkness.

Core Gameplay

Fearless revolves around the strategic use of light to navigate an otherwise dangerous environment.

The majority of each level is covered in darkness. Seika can survive brief exposure, but remaining outside a safe zone for too long will result in her death.

01

Light & Darkness

Navigate between illuminated areas to survive the darkness surrounding each level.

02

Portable Torches

Carry torches to provide a movable source of light and create opportunities for safe traversal.

03

Stationary Lamps

Place torches into lamps to extend their illumination range at the cost of mobility.

04

Key Transportation

Seika can carry either a key or a torch. Players must plan their route around this restriction to bring the key to the exit.
