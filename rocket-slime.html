Rocket Slime

A physics-based platformer built around movement and precision.

About the Game

Rocket Slime is a physics-based platformer where the player propels a slime through each level using a crosshair-based movement system.

The goal is to navigate through the level and reach the flag using as few jumps as possible.

My Role

Rocket Slime was developed by a four-person team over three months. I served as the team lead while also contributing to the game's gameplay programming.

Team Lead

I led the four-person development team throughout the three-month project.

Gameplay Programming

I worked on the physics behind the slime character's movement and contributed to the game's save system.

Gameplay

Movement is controlled through a crosshair surrounding the slime. When the crosshair is red, clicking propels the slime in the opposite direction.

When the crosshair is grey, the player cannot launch the slime. Players must use the movement system to navigate the level and reach the flag efficiently.

Controls

A / D Move horizontally
Left Mouse Button Launch the slime in the opposite direction
Z Zoom out
ESC Open the pause menu
